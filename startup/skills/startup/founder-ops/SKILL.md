# Founder Ops

## Purpose

Keep the company operationally credible while the product and market work move fast. Reduce founder chaos in the areas where sloppiness compounds.

## Operating Frame

- Treat jurisdiction-specific legal work as high stakes and verification heavy.
- Distinguish document drafting from legal sufficiency.
- Keep equity, compliance, and governance decisions traceable to dated assumptions.
- Label major claims as evidenced, inferred, or provisional.

## Minimum Inputs

- Jurisdiction and entity status
- Request type such as legal, equity, governance, or compliance
- Existing agreements, cap table, or policy context
- Required reviewer or approver

If critical facts are missing, ask for the single highest-leverage unknown or mark the assumption as provisional and continue.

## Workflow

1. Classify the request: legal drafting, equity and cap table, governance, compliance, or recurring founder administration.
2. Capture jurisdiction, entity status, financing status, existing agreements, and who must review the output. If these facts are missing, surface them before making specific legal claims.
3. Draft the minimum useful artifact: checklist, decision memo, cap-table scenario, agreement outline, board pack, or compliance calendar.
4. For equity work, record ownership assumptions, vesting logic, dilution effects, and the founder behavior this structure is meant to encourage or prevent.
5. For compliance work, turn obligations into a dated tracker with owner, deadline, evidence required, and consequence of failure.
6. End every task with an escalation map: what is safe to proceed with internally, what needs counsel, and what should not be decided from a template alone.

## Required Outputs

- Requested operating artifact
- Assumption log
- Review and escalation map
- Compliance or governance tracker when relevant

## Default Output Format

Use this section order unless the user needs a legal memo, checklist, or cap-table model:

1. Request classification
2. Governing facts and assumptions
3. Draft artifact or structured checklist
4. Risks and review gates
5. Escalation map
6. Ongoing tracker if needed
7. Recommended next skill

## Verification Gate

Do not claim a document is legally sufficient without the governing facts, cited source material, or qualified counsel review. Mark jurisdiction-specific guidance as provisional when those conditions are missing.

## Handoff

- Use `$fundraising-kit` when founder-ops work feeds investor readiness.
- Loop back to `$founder-foundation` when operating decisions expose unresolved founder alignment issues.
