# Fundraising Kit

## Purpose

Package the company for investors without laundering uncertainty into fake confidence. Make every major claim traceable to evidence or clearly labeled inference.

## Operating Frame

- Build the story from proof upward, not from slogans downward.
- Separate investor-ready facts from founder optimism.
- Treat projections as assumption stacks that must be inspectable.
- Label major claims as evidenced, inferred, or aspirational.

## Minimum Inputs

- Round type and use of funds
- Investor profile
- Current traction and proof points
- Known weak spots in market, product, team, or metrics

If critical facts are missing, ask for the single highest-leverage unknown or mark the assumption as provisional and continue.

## Workflow

1. Check fundraising readiness. Clarify round type, use of funds, target investor profile, traction stage, and the main objection likely to appear first.
2. Build a proof inventory covering problem evidence, customer proof, product proof, go-to-market proof, team proof, and financial proof.
3. Draft the deck narrative in this order: pain, wedge, proof, market, product, traction, business model, team, use of funds, and why now.
4. For market sizing and financial projections, show the arithmetic, assumptions, and sensitivity points. Do not hide behind round numbers or unsupported benchmarks.
5. Create a diligence gap list. Record every slide claim that needs backup data, every legal or data-room item still missing, and every risky assumption an investor will attack.
6. Prepare companion materials as needed: memo, FAQ, diligence checklist, or term-sheet questions.

## Required Outputs

- Fundraising narrative
- Deck outline with proof status on each major claim
- Projection assumptions
- Diligence gap list
- Investor objection map

## Default Output Format

Use this section order unless the user asks for a deck, memo, or diligence room checklist:

1. Raise context
2. Proof inventory
3. Narrative summary
4. Deck outline with claim status
5. Projection assumptions and sensitivities
6. Investor objection map
7. Diligence gaps
8. Recommended next skill

## Verification Gate

Do not mark this complete while any important claim is both central to the raise and unsupported by evidence. Tag each claim as evidenced, inferred, or aspirational, and surface the highest-risk gaps instead of smoothing them over.

- Use `$gtm-launch` or `$growth-engine` to improve weak proof areas before the raise.
- Use `$founder-ops` to tighten cap-table, legal, and diligence hygiene.
